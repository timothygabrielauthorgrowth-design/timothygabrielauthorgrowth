FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly do you do?

We are book marketing and promotion specialists helping authors sell more books, engage readers, and build brands through customized strategy including launches, promotion, Amazon optimization, social media, email marketing, reader engagement, branding, and advertising.

Do you guarantee sales?

We guarantee structured strategy and professional execution. We focus on measurable growth with transparent reporting, relentless optimization, and honest communication at every stage.

How is communication handled?

Everything via professional email. Every strategy, update, report, and decision is documented for complete transparency, accountability, and permanent record keeping.

Can you help already published books?

Absolutely. Many authors come to us months or years after publishing. It is never too late for proper marketing. We have revived and reignited sales for dormant books successfully.

Can you help before publication?

Yes, and we strongly recommend it. Pre-launch marketing is one of the most powerful things you can do for a successful release.

Who is Sue Strachan?

Sue Strachan is an experienced publishing and literary professional who partners with Timothy Gabriel. She strengthens publishing direction and literary positioning while Timothy handles marketing strategy and execution.

How much do services cost?

Pricing varies by scope, complexity, and duration. We offer solutions for various budgets with clear scoping, no hidden fees, and documented agreements. Email us for a customized quote.

Do you work internationally?

Yes. All work is via email so geography is not a barrier. We support authors worldwide.

How quickly can you start?

We respond within 24 to 48 hours. Discovery begins immediately and strategies are typically ready within one to two weeks. Time-sensitive projects can be expedited.

What makes you different?

Exclusive book specialization, custom strategies, email-documented accountability, focus on real metrics (sales, reviews, revenue), long-term engagement, selective client acceptance, and publishing expertise through Sue Strachan.
